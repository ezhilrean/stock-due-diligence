# Definition of Done

A report is ready only when each applicable item is complete or explicitly marked incomplete.

## Identity and scope
- [ ] Legal name, ticker, exchange, share class, and jurisdiction verified.
- [ ] Analysis date, price date, currency, and financial periods stated.
- [ ] Ambiguity resolved or disclosed.

## Evidence
- [ ] Material claims have traceable sources.
- [ ] Primary filings, exchange disclosures, regulator records, and court records prioritized where relevant.
- [ ] Time-sensitive information includes dates.
- [ ] No invented sources, searches, citations, or figures.
- [ ] Conflicts and limitations disclosed.
- [ ] Facts, allegations, interpretations, and unknowns distinguished.

## Financials and valuation
- [ ] Periods and currencies are consistent where possible.
- [ ] Cash flow, debt, liquidity, and accounting quality considered.
- [ ] Auditor opinion and material restatements checked.
- [ ] Valuation methods fit the business and assumptions are visible.
- [ ] Downside scenarios and sensitivity discussed.

## Risk investigation
- [ ] Legal and regulatory matters include status, date, and outcome where available.
- [ ] Allegations are not described as proven facts without evidence.
- [ ] Cybersecurity claims are supported; outages are not equated with malware.
- [ ] Governance, related-party transactions, disclosure quality, and shareholder rights considered.
- [ ] Material gaps and open questions are visible.

## Conclusion
- [ ] Summary identifies key positives and material risks.
- [ ] Conclusion is qualified and evidence-based.
- [ ] No guarantees or unsupported personalized buy/sell instructions.
- [ ] Conditions that could change the assessment are recorded.
- [ ] Source log and review triggers included.

If a critical item cannot be completed, disclose the limitation and use `Further research` rather than implying the company passed the check.
