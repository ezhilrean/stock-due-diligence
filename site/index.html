Better stock research starts with better evidence.

Stock Due Diligence helps structure research into publicly traded companies, including financial health, legal and regulatory matters, governance, valuation assumptions, and downside scenarios.

Open ChatGPTView source repository

Independent research aid. Not investment, legal, tax, or personalized financial advice.

Financial quality

Review revenue, profitability, cash flow, debt, liquidity, accounting quality, and dilution.

Legal and governance

Organize court records, regulatory actions, allegations, disclosure quality, leadership, and related-party risks.

Valuation and downside

State assumptions, compare scenarios, identify evidence gaps, and record what could change the conclusion.

Source discipline

Prioritize primary sources, cite material claims, distinguish allegations from findings, and label unknowns.

Evidence before conclusions

A lawsuit or allegation is not proof of wrongdoing. Missing public evidence is not proof that a risk does not exist. Reports should state their as-of date, sources reviewed, uncertainties, and further research needed.
