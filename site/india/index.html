Financial results review checklist

For each company, review the latest quarterly and annual results, preferably consolidated statements where relevant. Compare the same period year-on-year and verify the reporting period and units in the filing.

PROFITABILITY
Revenue and earnings

Record revenue/total income, operating profit or EBITDA when reported, profit after tax (PAT), and basic/diluted EPS.

FINANCIAL HEALTH
Balance sheet and cash flow

Review total assets, borrowings, cash, operating cash flow, capital expenditure, and free cash flow where disclosed.

COMPARABILITY
Periods and basis

Keep consolidated and standalone figures separate. Note exceptional items, restatements, audit status, and whether figures are quarterly or year-to-date.
